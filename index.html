The Paradigm Shift

From Data Gatherer
to Agent Manager

For decades, senior estimators spent 80% of their time hunting through PDFs, doing takeoffs, and organizing documents. They were hired for their brains but used for manual labor.

We believe it’s time for a promotion. With GuthrieAI handling the heavy lifting, your estimators don't need to "do the work", they need to manage the agents doing the work.

What an Agent Manager does:

  • Reviews AI Summaries: Instead of reading 100-page RFPs, you review a clean, 1-page executive summary of the true scope.
  • Applies Strategic Pricing: You stop counting bolts and start applying your market knowledge, margins, and risk analysis.
  • Closes the Deal: You use the time saved to build client relationships, negotiate, and win more profitable work.
Who Does What

Client, Agent Manager and AI Apprentice

A clear division of labor. You remain in control of the strategy and the final decision, while the AI handles the heavy lifting and your Agent Manager ensures accuracy.

What the client does

  • Reviews the scope of work (30-minute report) and decides Bid or Not bid
  • Clicks Run takeoff
  • Chooses who reviews the AI decisions, or reviews them itself
  • Chooses Budget or Quote for each vendor family group
  • Reviews pricing

What the Agent Manager does

  • Reviews AI decisions when chosen: valid, update or send to RFI
  • Quotes on behalf of partner vendors

What the AI Apprentice does

  • Drafts the 30-minute report and scope of work from the ITB
  • Runs the full takeoff in about an hour
  • Logs every decision it makes for review
  • Learns from corrected approaches in the AI decisions database
  • Learns from Bid / Not bid choices to help select future projects
  • Drafts RFIs for the GC
  • Updates the takeoff with GC answers to RFIs
  • Generates outputs: part list, schedules, sizes, RFQ
  • Sends RFQs to vendors
  • Prices budgeted groups from the filled quotes database
  • Levels quotes once all are received
  • Generates the standard bidform and proposal
  • Converts them into the client's format
  • Submits the bid to the GC and follows up
  • Records the result: Won or Lost
