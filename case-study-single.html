Overview

As one of the PA’s leading design and build workplace specialists, Company X delivers high-end commercial office transformations under tight turnarounds. With complex projects requiring over 100 design sheets passed between client, design, and cost management teams, traditional review processes were vulnerable to revision drift and miscommunication.

"Guthrie AI has transformed our design review workflows. Having a single source of truth for drawing markups and automatic RFI extraction eliminated duplicated effort and saved us from a potential £150,000 site error."

— Senior Design & Preconstruction Manager, Company X

The Challenge

Before implementing Guthrie AI, Company X faced significant administrative overhead during preconstruction. Drawing packs running up to 100 sheets passed through roughly eight rounds of review. Markups were scattered across PDF emails and meeting notes, leading to:

  • Uncoordinated dimension conflicts between architectural and MEP sheets.
  • Hours spent manually tracking RFI deadlines and scope changes.
  • High risk of scope gaps reaching sub-contractors on site.
Company X design team coordinating multi-trade specifications using centralized digital markups.

The Solution: Centralised Preconstruction AI

By deploying Guthrie AI’s Virtual Bid Assistants and automated drawing comparison routines, Company X established one authoritative set of markups for all downstream teams. The AI automatically parses submittals, extracts line items, and cross-checks revision sets in real time.
